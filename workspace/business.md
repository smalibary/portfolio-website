## Business Name

**The Design Eye / عين التصميم** — personal brand and content platform of Salem Malibary.

## What It Does

Salem is building a mass-appeal authority brand in the built-environment space for an Arabic-speaking audience. He reveals hidden mechanics behind objects, rooms, buildings, and cities — using behavioural science, systems thinking, and AI as working method. The brand is in Phase 1 (voice-finding via LinkedIn curation). Revenue is not active yet; the strategy is audience-first, premium-tier monetisation downstream. The endgame: high-fee advisory retainers, books, paid speaking, and eventually his own architecture + real-estate consulting office — all funded by audience credibility and a 15M SAR capital unlock at 1M SAR/year revenue.

## Known Workflows

> List things you already do repeatedly — even if they're not documented yet.

1. **LinkedIn post creation** — find source → verify scope → analyse hidden mechanic → draft 6-beat post → review voice → publish. Target 2-3/week. Roughly 2-3 hours per post.
2. **Academic alignment cycle** — content topic → paper topic → teaching material → back to content. Same inputs serve three outputs.
3. **Website development** — building and iterating the Jaspr-based personal site (`website-jaspr/`). Design system, content management, admin panel work.
4. **Context file maintenance** — updating `context/` files (identity, strategy, decisions log) when direction shifts. Append-only decision log.
5. **PhD research** — ongoing thesis work at University of Sydney (IEQ Lab). Competes for time with content and site work.

## Top Pains

> What's broken, frustrating, or slow right now?

1. **Time scarcity** — solo operator juggling academic job (KAU lecturer), PhD research, content creation, and website development. Everything competes for the same person's time. Capacity: ~5-10 hours/week for content after academic duties.
2. **Voice still forming** — Phase 1 (voice-finding). Only ~3 months of LinkedIn posting. Voice is sharpening but not yet consistent across 30+ posts (the phase exit signal).
3. **No revenue yet** — pre-revenue. No products, no services, no email list, no payment infrastructure. Everything is investment with no financial return yet.
4. **Pattern of system-building over execution** — documented in decisions log (2026-03-08): tendency to build elaborate systems instead of shipping content. Actively guarded against.

## Current Goals

> What are you optimizing for in the next 3-6 months?

1. **Sharpen voice on LinkedIn** — reach the Phase 1 exit signal: consistent voice across 30+ posts, a few posts that performed unexpectedly well, 4-5 themes generating genuine interest, inbound DMs from the right kinds of people.
2. **Ship and iterate the personal website** — the Jaspr site is in active development. Get it deployed, refine the design system, add content routes.
3. **Maintain academic alignment** — keep content topics feeding papers, papers fueling promotion, classes generating research data. Target 2-3 papers/year from this ecosystem.
